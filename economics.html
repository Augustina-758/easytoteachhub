SS1 ECONOMICS Topic: Introduction to Economics Learning Objectives At the end of the lesson, students should be able to: 1. Define Economics. 2. Explain the meaning of scarcity. 3. Explain the meaning of choice. 4. Explain opportunity cost. 5. State the importance of Economics in everyday life. Meaning of Economics Economics is the study of how people use limited resources to satisfy their unlimited wants. People have many things they want, such as food, clothing, shelter, education, transportation, and entertainment. However, the resources available to satisfy these wants are limited. Because resources are limited, people must make choices. Important Economic Terms 1. Wants Wants are the things people desire to have or enjoy. Examples include food, clothes, a phone, education, and a house. 2. Scarcity Scarcity means that resources are limited while human wants are unlimited. For example, a student may want new clothes, a new phone, and new shoes, but may not have enough money to buy everything. 3. Choice Choice means selecting one thing from two or more alternatives. For example, if a student has ₦2,000 and wants both a textbook and a pair of shoes but cannot afford both, the student has to choose one. 4. Opportunity Cost Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative that is given up when a choice is made. For example, if a student uses ₦2,000 to buy a textbook instead of a pair of shoes, the pair of shoes becomes the opportunity cost. Importance of Economics Economics helps people to: - Make good decisions about how to use their money. - Understand how prices are determined. - Use scarce resources wisely. - Understand production and consumption. - Make better choices in everyday life. - Understand economic problems in society. Summary Human wants are unlimited, but the resources available to satisfy those wants are limited. This creates scarcity. Because of scarcity, people must make choices, and every choice involves an opportunity cost. Class Exercise 1. What is Economics? 2. What is scarcity? 3. Explain choice. 4. Define opportunity cost. 5. Give one example of opportunity cost in your daily life. Homework Explain the relationship between scarcity, choice, and opportunity cost.
