Sample Lesson Note

SS1 English Language — Parts of Speech

Objectives: By the end of the lesson, learners should be able to define parts of speech, identify common parts of speech and use them correctly in sentences.

Content: Noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction and interjection.

Class Activity: Identify the parts of speech in selected sentences.

Evaluation: Give examples of five parts of speech and use each in a sentence.
